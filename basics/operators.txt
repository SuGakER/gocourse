Logical Operators:

! (logical NOT) !true = false/ !zero = 1
|| (logical OR) a > b || a > c || c > g
&& (logical AND) a > b && a > c || c > d

Bitwise Operators: 
& (bitwise AND)
| (bitwise OR) 
^ (bitwise XOR)
&^ (bitwise, AND NOT)
<< (left shift)
>> (right shift)

Comparison Operators:
== (equal) (quality between two values)
!= (not equal) (not equality between values - use exclamation mark+ equal symbol)
< (less than)
<= (less or equal to)
 > (greater than)
 >= (greater than or equal to)
